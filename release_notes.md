## Phase 7: Menu Bar Battery & AppBeBack

- **Battery Status:** The generic DeskBar icon in the macOS menu bar has been replaced by a beautiful, dynamic battery gauge and percentage display. 
- **AppBeBack Functionality:** The menu bar dropdown is now supercharged. It actively tracks recently closed applications (retaining up to 10 apps) and displays them in a new "Recently Closed" submenu. Clicking any of them instantly restores the application just like the classic AppBeBack utility.
